The Edge of Stillness

By Ryan Giordano @ryangiordanophotographyco

This photograph was shot in The Blue Lagoon located in the island of Malta in the Mediterranean Sea in the fall of 2023. The Edge of Stillness shows party goers on a day cruise which meets the waters quiet and still waters of the Blue Lagoon. Then the edge which is the separation of the quiet waters and the party goers get broken, the party goers are then in the Lagoon. This then embodies them in the environment of the Blue Lagoon surrounded by the cliffs and the sea life in the Lagoon.

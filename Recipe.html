Alcoholic lab is a place where I take notes of the drinks that I developed for fun or ones I found interesting. All the drinks below are not guaranteed to be legit nor safe. They are just for hobby purposes. If you have any suggestions, please send me an email and we can talk!

Note: all drinks can be made alcohol-free

~ BALBOA MILKTEA (WHITE RUSSIAN TWIST) ~

Fresh Milk 30ml

Homemade Earl Gray Vodka 45ml

Kahlua Coffee Liquer 15ml

Simple Syrup 30ml

Boba

A bold reinvention of the classic milk tea, infused with the elegance of earl gray vodka for a deeper, richer flavor. Enhanced with the aromatic touch of Kahlua Coffee Liqueur, this cocktail brings a perfect balance of creamy sweetness and a warm, subtle kick. Named after San Diego's historic Balboa Park, it evokes a sense of exploration and sophistication, with a playful nod to the beloved "boba".

~ EARL GREY SOUR (DAIQUIRI TWIST) ~

Homemade Earl Grey Vodka 45ml

Lime Juice 25ml

Simple Syrup 15ml

Born at the acclaimed Sober Company, once a jewel among Asia's Best 50 Bars, this cocktail experienced its prime before fading from the limelight. We've meticulously reverse-engineered this classic to revive one of our all-time favorites. Experience a harmonious blend of sweet and sour, accented with refreshing notes of lime and the deeply infused elegance of Earl Grey tea. A timeless toast to the past, reborn for your enjoyment.

~ BLACK'S BEACH ICED TEA (LONG ISLAND ICED TEA TWIST) ~

Scottish Gin 30ml

Tequila 15ml

Vodka 15ml

White Rum 15ml

Triple Sec 15ml

Simple Syeup 20ml

Lime Juice 25ml

Cola 1 dash

Inspired by the serene sunsets at Black's Beach in La Jolla, this cocktail delivers a bold twist on the classic Long Island Iced Tea. Infused with robust Scottish gin, it offers a deeper, herbal profile that sets it apart. A splash of fresh lime juice adds a crisp, revitalizing note, evoking the cool, breezy evenings by the shore.

~ JOHNNY SILVERHAND ~

Tequila 20ml

Overproof Rum 25ml

Cointreau/Triple Sec 30ml

Blue Agave Syrup 15ml

Chili Pepper 15ml

A bold, sweet, and strong cocktail that packs a punch from the very first sip. Infused with an unexpected kick of spice, this drink is inspired by Johnny Silverhand, the legendary lone wolf punk star of Cyberpunk 2077. The unique blend of sweet and fiery flavors captures the essence of his rebellious spirit and enigmatic presence.

~ JADE GRAPE JUICE ~

Fresh Grapes 7-12

Irish Whisky 30ml

Rum 45ml

Cointreau/Triple Sec 30ml

A delicate blend of mild sweetness, subtle spice, and the zest of fresh grapefruit, inspired by the legendary ancient Chinese drink praised in the verses of the great Poet Li Bai. This cocktail evokes the poetic spirit and timeless elegance of his works, inviting you to sip and savor the essence of history and myth.
